Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Fusion Tech Dynamics (Private) Limited, trading as Fusion Tech Dynamics, based in Islamabad, Pakistan. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Fusion Tech Dynamics.

What this policy covers

This policy covers this website only. It does not cover ReCura, Cubbo, Orvex or any other product or app, which have or will have their own terms and privacy information.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the topic you select and your message.
  • Careers form: your name, email address, the role or internship you are applying for, your university or company, an optional link to your CV or portfolio, and your message.
  • Email or phone: whatever you include when you contact us directly.

Please do not send us sensitive information, such as health or financial details, through these forms.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss the work or training you asked about.
  • To review your job or internship application and contact you about it.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you have asked us to.

Services that help run this website

Some outside services are involved in running this website:

  • FormSubmit receives what you enter in our forms and forwards it to our email inbox.
  • Cloudflare Email Routing forwards messages sent to our email address to the mailbox where we read and store them.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and may process technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect it.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs supply the fonts and icons on our pages, so your browser connects to them and shares your IP address.

These services may process data outside Pakistan and handle it under their own privacy policies. If you follow our link to LinkedIn, LinkedIn's own policy applies.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiries for up to 2 years and job or internship applications for up to 1 year, unless we need them longer for an ongoing project, a contract or a legal requirement.

Keeping information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No website or email system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it or delete it. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can, and may need to keep some information where the law requires it. To make a request, email contact@fusiontechdynamics.com.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them through it. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
