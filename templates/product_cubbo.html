Cubbo

The friend who talks back.

A screen-free AI talking teddy bear for young children, speaking Urdu and English. Cubbo is a prototype and is not on sale yet.

Follow Cubbo's Progress All Products
Cubbo is a working name.
The idea

Play and learning, without another screen.

Many parents want to limit screen time, and few talking toys speak Urdu. Cubbo gives children something to talk to in the language they use at home.

What Cubbo does

A conversation, a story, a new word

Children speak to Cubbo and it answers aloud, in Urdu or English.

01

Talks

Holds a spoken conversation, with no screen for the child to look at.

02

Plays & teaches

Tells stories, sings rhymes, practises counting and the alphabet, and teaches a new word each day.

03

Answers to parents

Parents choose the language and set daily time limits from an app.
