ReCura

Private patient tracking between visits.

A connected doctor and patient platform that keeps each patient's treatment history in one place. We are starting with aesthetic dermatology, and designing it to extend to other specialties.

The problem

Care continues after the patient leaves the clinic.

Treatment photos sit in phone galleries, notes sit on paper, and patients can drift away between sessions. That makes progress harder to see and follow-up easier to miss.

How it works

One record from first visit to latest session

Each visit adds to a timeline the clinician can review with the patient.

01

Record

Capture a photo at each visit. On-device analysis measures redness and affected area alongside the clinician's own notes.

02

Compare

See the visit timeline, before-and-after photos and a treatment response chart.

03

Stay connected

Send patients a private photo link by WhatsApp or QR code, and bring back those overdue for a visit.
