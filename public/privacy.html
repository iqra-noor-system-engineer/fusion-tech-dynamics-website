Who we are

Fusion Tech Dynamics is an engineering company based in Islamabad, Pakistan. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • The details you enter in the enquiry form: your name, email address, the disciplines you select and your message.
  • Your email address if you sign up for product updates.
  • The content of any email or call you start with us.

How we use it

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry and, if you asked for them, to send product updates. We do not sell your information.

Products we build

Our own products, such as ReCura, have their own privacy terms inside the product. For example, ReCura encrypts patient names, notes and photos on the clinic's device before they are saved.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses only essential cookies needed for it to work, and standard, privacy-friendly analytics to understand overall visits, such as which pages are viewed. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell or share this information with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond to you or to deliver a project you start with us, and then delete them. Product-update sign-ups are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us. You can unsubscribe from product updates at any time.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Questions

For anything about this page, email contact@fusiontechdynamics.com or call +92 347 5370806. Fusion Tech Dynamics, Islamabad, Pakistan.
