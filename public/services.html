Mechanical design

Enclosures and physical products designed alongside the electronics, ready for manufacture.

  • Industrial design and 3D CAD
  • Embedded-product enclosures and mounting
  • Thermal, tolerance and assembly considerations
  • 3D printing, CNC and rapid prototyping
  • Injection-moulded part design
  • Draft, wall thickness, ribs, bosses and snap fits
  • DFM review and manufacturing handover
  • Prototype-to-production design iteration

Electronics & PCB design

Schematics, layout and bring-up for digital, analogue, power and mixed-signal boards, up to 8 layers.

  • Architecture, schematics and BOM optimisation
  • High-speed interfaces and controlled impedance
  • Power integrity, grounding and EMC-aware layout
  • DFM, DFA and design-review packages
  • Prototype bring-up and fault diagnosis
  • Manufacturing files, test points and fixtures
  • Library and lifecycle management
  • 2-, 4-, 6- and 8-layer boards

  • IoT gateways, MQTT, BLE, Wi-Fi and cellular
  • Edge-AI embedded products

Aerospace, UAV & test

Avionics integration and verification for unmanned aircraft, proven on hardware-in-the-loop test benches.
