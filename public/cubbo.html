Cubbo

The friend who talks back.

A screen-free AI talking teddy bear for young children, speaking Urdu and English. Cubbo is a prototype and is not on sale yet.

The idea

Play and learning, without another screen.

Many parents want to limit screen time, and few talking toys speak Urdu. Cubbo gives children something to talk to in the language they use at home.

What Cubbo does

A conversation, a story, a new word.

Everything happens by voice, in Urdu or English, with parents setting the rules.

Talks

A real conversation

Holds a spoken conversation, with no screen for the child to look at.

Plays & teaches

Stories and rhymes

Tells stories, sings rhymes, practises counting and the alphabet, and teaches a new word each day.

Answers to parents

Parents in control

Parents choose the language and set daily time limits from an app.

Made for home

Designed around children and parents.

Cubbo is a working name. The prototype voice pipeline completed its first end-to-end test in August 2026.

Urdu & English

Speaks both languages, so children can use the one they know.

Screen-free

All voice, with nothing to watch or scroll.

Parent controls

Language and daily limits are set from an app.

Still in development

We are building and testing the prototype before any sale.
