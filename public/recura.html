Encrypted on your device

Patient names, phone numbers, notes and photos are locked with your password. Our servers only store the scrambled version, so we can't read them.

Not encrypted

Treatment or condition names, dates, visit scores and payment amounts stay readable, so the app can sort, chart and remind you.

Your recovery key matters

You get one recovery key when you register. If you lose both it and your password, nobody can recover that data, including us.
