Hero background video
=====================

Drop your homepage hero video here as:  hero.mp4

Recommended specs:
- Muted, 5-10 second seamless loop
- 1920x1080 (16:9), H.264 MP4
- Under ~5 MB  (it autoplays on every visit, so compress it)

It will automatically play behind the homepage hero.
Until a file named hero.mp4 exists here, the hero shows the drone
image (images/hero-drone.jpg) as a poster fallback — so nothing breaks.

To swap to a YouTube/Vimeo hero instead, send the link and it can be
embedded differently.
